Questions?

Clear answers for professional workflows.

How accurate is the conversion?

add
The neural network excels at clean, polyphonic piano and guitar tracks. Complex, heavily layered mixes with intense percussion may produce noisier MIDI data requiring manual cleanup.

Is my data safe?

add
Absolutely. Amber Sonic uses WebAssembly to run the AI model directly in your browser. Audio files are never uploaded to our servers; the conversion happens entirely on your local CPU/GPU.

Which DAWs are supported?

add
The output is a standard Type 0 MIDI (.mid) file. This format is universally supported by Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Cubase, and virtually all other digital audio workstations.
