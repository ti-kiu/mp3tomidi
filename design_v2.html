Technical Inquiries

Frequently asked questions about Amber Sonic.

How accurate is the conversion? expand_more

Our engine achieves high polyphonic pitch detection accuracy by using the Basic Pitch neural network architecture. It excels at separating simultaneous notes, though results may vary with heavy background noise or extremely distorted timbres.

Is my data safe? expand_more

Completely. Unlike many cloud-based tools, Amber Sonic performs the conversion within your browser environment. Your original audio files are never uploaded to any remote server.

Which DAWs are supported? expand_more

The exported MIDI files use standard format conventions compatible with all major Digital Audio Workstations, including Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Cubase, Pro Tools, and Reaper.
