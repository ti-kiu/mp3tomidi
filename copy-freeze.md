**Q: How accurate is the MP3 to MIDI conversion?**
A: Accuracy depends on the audio quality and complexity. Simple melodies with clear instruments convert best. Single-instrument recordings produce the most accurate results. Complex mixes may need manual cleanup in a DAW.

**Q: Can I convert vocals to MIDI?**
A: Yes, you can convert vocal recordings to MIDI. However, vocal transcription is less accurate than instrument transcription. Simple melodies work better than complex vocal arrangements.

**Q: What audio formats are supported?**
A: We support MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, and M4A. WAV files typically produce the best results due to their lossless quality.

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No signup, no credit limits, no file size limits, no watermarks. The tool runs entirely in your browser.

**Q: Can I edit the converted MIDI file?**
A: Yes, the downloaded .mid file is a standard MIDI file that can be edited in any DAW (FL Studio, Ableton, Logic Pro, GarageBand, etc.) or MIDI editor.

**Q: Does it work on mobile?**
A: Yes, the converter works on mobile browsers. However, desktop browsers provide a better experience for larger files and more precise editing.

**Q: Is my audio uploaded to a server?**
A: No. All processing happens in your browser using TensorFlow.js. Your audio files never leave your device.

**Q: What is MIDI?**
A: MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) is a protocol that stores musical information like notes, timing, and velocity. Unlike audio files, MIDI files contain instructions for playing music, making them easy to edit and rearrange.
