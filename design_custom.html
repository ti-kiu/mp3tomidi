QUESTIONS
FAQ
How accurate is the conversion?
Basic Pitch achieves high accuracy on clear, single-instrument recordings. Full mixes with multiple overlapping instruments may produce extra notes that need cleanup in your DAW.
Is my audio uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser using TensorFlow.js. Your audio files never leave your device.
What audio formats are supported?
MP3, WAV, OGG, and FLAC. Any format supported by the browser's Audio API works.
Can I use the MIDI commercially?
Yes. The downloaded .mid file is yours. Just make sure you have rights to the original audio.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, but desktop browsers provide a better experience for large files. Mobile processing may be slower on older devices.
