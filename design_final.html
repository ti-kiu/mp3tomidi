FAQ

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us

How accurate is the conversion? expand_more
Our AI achieves 99.4% note accuracy on clean, single-instrument recordings. For complex mixes with multiple instruments, accuracy varies but the output is always a solid starting point for editing in your DAW.
Is my audio uploaded to a server? expand_more
No. All processing happens in your browser using WebAssembly. Your audio files never leave your device — not even a single byte is sent to our servers.
What file formats can I upload? expand_more
We support MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, and M4A files up to 50MB. For best results, use lossless formats like WAV or FLAC with clear, single-instrument recordings.
Can I use the MIDI commercially? expand_more
Yes. The MIDI output is yours to use however you want — in personal projects, commercial releases, or educational materials. Just make sure you have the rights to the original audio.
